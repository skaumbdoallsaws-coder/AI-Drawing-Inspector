# Area 08: Execution and Vision

## Schedule

- Start: August 13, 2026
- End: August 14, 2026
- Time: 2 days, 4 hours

## What This Area Must Address

This area connects demonstrated execution to a focused one-year plan and a large long-term outcome. It must show speed and ambition without treating every prototype workflow as an immediate priority.

The work must address:

- What has been built and by whom.
- Development velocity and major technical decisions.
- What has been learned recently.
- Progress since submitting the YC application.
- The largest product, customer, and company risks.
- The next pilot and product milestones.
- How YC funding and the batch would change execution.
- The one-year operating target.
- The long-term product-intelligence vision.
- Why the initial wedge can expand into a venture-scale company.

## Core Questions

- How far along are you?
- What did you build most recently?
- What have you learned from users?
- What is the biggest risk?
- What are the next three milestones?
- What would you do with YC funding?
- Where will the company be in one year?
- What does InspectorPro become if it works?
- Why can this become a billion-dollar company?

## Daily Plan

### August 13: Execution and Near-Term Plan

- Build a dated product-progress timeline.
- Identify the strongest evidence of speed and judgment.
- Define the next three milestones and largest risks.
- State exactly how funding changes the plan.

### August 14: Vision and Expansion Logic

- Explain the path from FAI and drawing checks to shared product intelligence.
- Connect each expansion step to data and users earned through the prior step.
- Describe the long-term company in plain language.
- Pressure-test whether the vision follows from the wedge.

## Deliverables

- Dated build and learning timeline.
- Since-application progress sheet.
- Next-three-milestones plan.
- Risk register with mitigation and evidence.
- YC funding-use summary.
- One-year target.
- Wedge-to-vision expansion map.

## Definition of Done

This area is complete when current execution is factual and dated, next steps are focused, and the long-term vision follows logically from the first customer workflow rather than from a list of unrelated possibilities.
