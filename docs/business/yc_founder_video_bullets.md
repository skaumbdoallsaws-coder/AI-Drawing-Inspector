## Talking-point bullets (~163 words ≈ 60s at an energetic pace)

- Hi, I'm Shadrack, founder of InspectorPro. I'm a mechanical and manufacturing engineer. My career has been around CAD, drawings, quality, and production problems.

- One thing I've seen over and over: product knowledge is never in one place. The CAD model says one thing, the drawing another, and the rest lives in people's heads.

- Real example: a supplier part comes back out of tolerance by a few hundredths of a millimeter. Three people, two days of back-and-forth to decide: accept it, rework it, or scrap it. The answer existed. It was just scattered.

- InspectorPro anchors AI to the CAD model, so engineering, quality, and purchasing get that answer in minutes.

- I built the working prototype myself. It checks drawings, reasons over 3D assemblies, and handles first-article dispositions.

- Long term: the product intelligence layer for companies that build physical products from CAD.

- I'm applying to YC because I can build. I want to get faster at customers, pilots, and turning this into a real company.

## Recording checklist

- 6–8 takes; pick the one with the most **energy**, not the cleanest one. A minor stumble reads as authentic.
- Pause one beat after **"The answer existed."** — it's the hook.
- Look at the **lens**, not the screen. Card at lens height.
- ~130–160 wpm. Decent light, quiet room, phone at eye level — nothing fancier.
- Say the YC line, stop, done. No "thanks for watching."
- Say "I," not "we" — solo and honest is a strength here.
- Don't say: "product-context AI platform," "AI agents," feature-mode names, market sizes, "revolutionize/disrupt," other funding conversations, "just a prototype."
