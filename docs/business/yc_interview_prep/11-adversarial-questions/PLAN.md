# Area 11: Adversarial Questions

## Schedule

- Start: August 17, 2026
- End: August 17, 2026
- Time: 1 day, 2 hours

## What This Area Must Address

This area tests the weakest assumptions rather than polishing favorable answers. The goal is calm, direct reasoning and honest acknowledgment of uncertainty.

## Required Challenges

- You have no meaningful traction. Why should we believe customers want this?
- Is InspectorPro a feature rather than a company?
- Why have you built so many workflows before getting customers?
- Why will Siemens, Dassault, PTC, or an AI vendor not copy this?
- Why can a manufacturer not use ChatGPT with its documents?
- How can anyone trust AI to recommend a manufacturing disposition?
- What happens when InspectorPro is wrong?
- Are proprietary CAD files safe with your model providers?
- Can this work on an unseen assembly or only the piston demonstration?
- Why will slow-moving manufacturers adopt a startup product?
- Why are you still a solo founder?
- What evidence would prove your thesis wrong?
- What is the single largest reason this company could fail?
- What will you do if YC rejects the application?

## Session Plan

- Rank objections by probability and severity.
- Answer each objection directly before defending the company.
- Separate current evidence from planned mitigation.
- Remove dismissive descriptions of competitors or customer concerns.
- Run two randomized challenge rounds with interruptions.
- Review only answers that became defensive, vague, or too long.

## Deliverables

- Ranked objection bank.
- Direct answer and evidence for each major objection.
- List of assumptions that remain unproven.
- Falsification criteria for the first pilots.
- Updated risk register.

## Definition of Done

This area is complete when Shadrack can discuss the strongest objections without becoming defensive, admit unknowns without losing command, and explain the next experiment that resolves each important uncertainty.
