# Area 03: Solution and Wedge

## Schedule

- Start: July 29, 2026
- End: August 1, 2026
- Time: 4 days, 8 hours

## What This Area Must Address

This area explains what InspectorPro does, how it works at a product level, and why the initial wedge is narrow enough to sell. It must prevent the product's many workflows from making the company appear unfocused.

The work must address:

- A jargon-free product description.
- The first paid workflow.
- How drawings, CAD, inspection data, and assembly context are connected.
- The user-visible magic moment.
- Why generic AI and existing systems cannot produce the same result reliably.
- What works today versus what is planned.
- What InspectorPro will deliberately not build during the first pilots.
- How the wedge expands into the long-term platform.

## Core Questions

- What does InspectorPro do?
- How does it solve the problem?
- What is the initial product wedge?
- Why drawing checks and FAI disposition first?
- What is fundamentally different?
- What is the magic moment?
- Is the piston workflow hardcoded?
- Does it work on unseen customer data?
- Why have you built multiple workflows already?
- What will you not build this year?

## Daily Plan

### July 29: Product Mechanism

- Explain ingestion, linking, reasoning, and response in plain language.
- Produce one-sentence, three-sentence, and technical descriptions.
- Separate product behavior from implementation detail.

### July 30: Initial Wedge

- Define the first paid workflow and customer outcome.
- Explain why it has urgency, accessible data, and measurable value.
- Define what is excluded from the first pilot.

### July 31: Differentiation and Proof

- Explain why documents alone are insufficient.
- Explain the value of geometry and assembly context.
- Identify what has been demonstrated and what still needs customer validation.

### August 1: Breadth Without Loss of Focus

- Explain how Training, process maps, and facility layouts relate to the same product-intelligence foundation.
- State why they are not the current go-to-market focus.
- Run solution questions with interruptions and follow-ups.

## Deliverables

- One-sentence company description.
- Product mechanism diagram.
- First-wedge statement.
- Current-versus-future capability table.
- Explicit not-building list for initial pilots.
- Magic-moment explanation.
- Differentiation explanation.
- Ten to fifteen direct-answer cards.

## Definition of Done

This area is complete when InspectorPro sounds like a focused company with a credible entry workflow, rather than a collection of demonstrations, while preserving a clear path to the broader product-intelligence platform.
