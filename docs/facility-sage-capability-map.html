Who owns each decision

Sage owns intent

Understand phrasing, classify the request, resolve semantic references, choose the allowed tool, sequence bounded work, and explain verified results.

Deterministic engines own geometry

Coordinates, polygons, paths, fit, collision checks, layout patterns, operations, canonical validation, fingerprints, diffs, and receipts.

The user owns authority

Requirements, authored policy values, anchors, approved profiles, conflict choices, human review, explicit Save, and Undo.

Sage cannot invent coordinates, regulatory clearances, vehicle or operator profiles, safety/compliance/throughput claims, or approve Save on the user's behalf.
