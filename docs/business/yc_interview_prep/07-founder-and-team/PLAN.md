## What This Area Must Address

This area explains why Shadrack is unusually suited to build InspectorPro and addresses the risks of a solo founder directly. Credentials should support the story, but evidence of building speed, domain judgment, and persistence should lead it.

The work must address:

- Mechanical and manufacturing engineering experience.
- Professional engineering license and engineering education.
- Direct experience with product-data and manufacturing workflows.
- Technical ability demonstrated by the working product.
- Why this problem matters personally now.
- Current full-time commitment and exact work history.
- Solo-founder strengths and constraints.
- Cofounder openness without appearing uncommitted.
- Hiring needs and missing skills.
- Persistence, sacrifices, and response to setbacks.

- Why are you the right founder?

- Why now in your life?
- Are you working full-time?
- Why are you solo?
- Are you looking for a cofounder?
- What skills are missing?
- Why will you not give up?
- What can you do faster than another founder?
