# Area 09: Application Consistency

## Schedule

- Start: August 15, 2026
- End: August 15, 2026
- Time: 1 day, 2 hours

## What This Area Must Address

This area reconciles every important statement across the submitted application, founder video, product demo, repository, and current product state. Interviewers may have the application open and may ask for verification after the interview.

The audit must address:

- Prototype availability versus self-service pilot readiness.
- Exact customer, demo, and pilot status.
- Product capabilities versus founder-assisted demonstrations.
- Start date, work hours, layoff date, and full-time commitment.
- Founder, contractor, and AI-tool contributions.
- Current model providers and technical stack.
- Pricing and market assumptions.
- Time-saving and cost-saving claims.
- Company formation, ownership, location, and cofounder plans.
- Progress that occurred after submission.

## Session Plan

- Extract every quantitative or verifiable claim from the application and videos.
- Mark each claim current, outdated, estimated, ambiguous, or unsupported.
- Replace outdated internal answer cards with current facts.
- Prepare short explanations for apparent inconsistencies.
- Assemble source evidence for numbers that may be verified.
- Identify updates that should be shared if an interview is offered.

## Deliverables

- Application claim ledger.
- Video claim ledger.
- Current fact sheet with dates.
- List of corrected or updated facts.
- Evidence references for important numbers.
- Direct answers for every apparent inconsistency.

## Definition of Done

This area is complete when every material claim can be explained consistently, no interest is described as a commitment, and Shadrack can state what changed since submission without hesitation.
