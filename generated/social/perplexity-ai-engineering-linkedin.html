Perplexity is model surprise.

Not just a training metric. It can help AI engineers test whether a model recognizes a domain's language.

One way to read it

lower = more predictable
Perplexity(x1...xn) = ( Πi=1n [1 / P(xi | x1...xi-1)] )1/n

Low perplexity

The model finds the text familiar and easier to predict.

High perplexity

The model is uncertain. Many next-token paths look plausible.

A cheap first check before trusting a model on your data

Test the model on CAD operations, inspection reports, quality records, or other domain-specific text before relying on it downstream.
