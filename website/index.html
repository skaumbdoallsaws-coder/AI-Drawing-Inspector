Also on the platform

Capabilities that build on the same connected context.

Deviation analysis Available

When a dimension is out of tolerance, InspectorPro checks the assembly — mate relationships, fit interfaces, downstream parts — and tells you whether the part will still function, what risk you carry if you accept it, and what to do next.

FAI reporting Available

Numbered balloons on the drawing and auto pass/fail evaluation as you type measurements. Enter “all bores measured 25.003” and eight cells fill at once. Print a clean report with header, characteristics table, and signature lines.

Part sourcing Available

Pulls the dimensions, material, and thread specs from the drawing and searches supplier catalogs. It knows the assembly context — what the part connects to — so the options it surfaces actually fit.
