# Area 04: Go-to-Market and Economics

## Schedule

- Start: August 2, 2026
- End: August 5, 2026
- Time: 4 days, 8 hours

## What This Area Must Address

This area explains how InspectorPro moves from a founder-assisted prototype to paid, repeatable customer deployments. Pricing and ROI must be based on a coherent pilot and customer model rather than broad market assumptions.

The work must address:

- Pilot scope, duration, setup, and success metrics.
- Customer acquisition and founder-led sales.
- Manufacturing buying and security review cycles.
- Initial pricing and what supports it.
- Customer ROI and time to value.
- Product delivery costs and gross-margin direction.
- Expansion from one workflow to department, site, and enterprise usage.
- The shortest path to the first annual contract.

## Core Questions

- How do you get the first ten customers?
- What exactly does a pilot include?
- How long does deployment take?
- What must the customer provide?
- How much will you charge and why?
- What is the customer's ROI?
- How long is the sales cycle?
- What makes a pilot convert?
- What does expansion look like?
- What are the model and support costs?

## Daily Plan

### August 2: Pilot Design

- Define the first pilot from data intake through final review.
- Specify responsibilities, success metrics, duration, and conversion decision.
- Define founder-assisted versus self-service work.

### August 3: Pricing and ROI

- Build low, expected, and high initial contract scenarios.
- Connect price to labor, delay, scrap, and throughput value.
- Label assumptions that still require customer validation.

### August 4: Acquisition and Sales Cycle

- Define target-account sourcing, outreach, demonstration, champion building, and close.
- Identify likely objections and procurement stages.
- Create a credible plan for the first ten customers.

### August 5: Economics and Expansion

- Estimate model, infrastructure, onboarding, and support costs.
- Explain department, site, supplier, and enterprise expansion.
- Run rapid questions covering pricing and sales skepticism.

## Deliverables

- Pilot one-pager.
- Pilot success and conversion criteria.
- Pricing hypothesis with assumptions.
- Customer ROI worksheet.
- First-ten-customer acquisition plan.
- Sales-cycle map.
- Initial unit-economics worksheet.
- Expansion model.

## Definition of Done

This area is complete when Shadrack can explain a credible path from today's prototype to the first paid contract, justify pricing without unsupported precision, and identify exactly which commercial assumptions still need testing.
