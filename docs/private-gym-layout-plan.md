## Concept

Retain the agreed 60 x 30 m concept, totaling 1,800 square metres. Make Private Gym a strength-led commercial-style gym with a substantial cardio area, functional training, member amenities, and a social lounge. The new library broadens the equipment mix rather than requiring a larger building.

The design direction combines the free-weight, machine, and powerlifting emphasis described by [Los Campeones](https://loscampeonesgym.com/) with the cardio and functional-training variety described by [LA Fitness](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/Amenities.aspx). This is inspiration, not a reproduction of either operator's floor plan or facility standards.

The 60 x 30 m brief comes from the prior planning discussion. No separate saved Private Gym plan was located in the project documentation or planning outputs checked; the allocations and quantities below are a proposed revision, not a claim about an earlier approved equipment schedule.

## Review Findings

- The expanded catalog now supports distinct lower-body, general-strength, cable, free-weight, and cardio areas. Avoid placing every machine in one undifferentiated grid.
- The 19 new assets are not 19 exercise machines. They include a floor tile, a pool table, benches, and a loaded triceps bar.
- Catalog dimensions describe visible CAD geometry, not operating envelopes. Bar travel, plate loading, mounting, entry, servicing, and user movement must be assessed before final spacing.
- The imported Mach1 and Model stations have generic identities. Keep them in flexible general-machine bays until their intended exercises and working sides are confirmed; do not infer a muscle-group assignment from their filenames.
- Earlier frame-only models are not substitutes for complete machines. Do not count them as equipped exercise stations in this revision.
- Keep the three latest excluded files out, as requested. Earlier unpublished or scale-held imports also remain excluded.

## Placement and Appearance

- Group racks and benches into usable training bays, with weight retrieval beside the bays rather than across the main circulation route.
- Orient seats, pads, controls, and machine entry points toward accessible lanes. Verify loading arms and moving parts separately; do not infer the working side from canonical axes alone.
- Keep treadmill dismount/rear space and machine operating envelopes outside through-routes. Resolve dimensions from the applicable equipment instructions before freezing the layout.
- Give cable stations access to each occupied working face. Adjacent equipment must not occupy cable travel or exercise space.
- Store loaded bars on a compatible support after checking fit. Do not leave them across walking routes or assume an arbitrary shelf is suitable.
- Reserve cue movement around the pool table, not just its CAD footprint. Keep it in the lounge and outside entrance queues and circulation.
- Use InspectorPro branding at reception and on a feature wall. Proposed finishes: light walls, dark neutral training flooring, restrained teal/blue accents, and contrasting zone signage. Do not recolor whole machines in ways that obscure pads, grips, or controls.
- Maintain enough overhead space for the approximately 2.836 m-high Asm1 rack and its intended use. Overall model height alone does not establish the required ceiling clearance.

## Flooring and Rendering

Use the new 20 mm-thick interlocking tile as the basis for a proposed 120 m2 patch inside the functional/mobility allocation. This is part of the 180 m2 zone, not an additional area.

Before calculating quantities or making an array, inspect the interlocking edges and establish the installed repeat pitch. The 500 x 500 mm outer bounding box is not proof of effective coverage per tile. Check edge cuts, boundary pieces, and transitions to adjacent floor levels.

Prove a small connected tile patch first, then expand using shared geometry/instancing where supported. Do not create thousands of independent CAD downloads or duplicate mesh resources. Keep visible exercise machines as their actual CAD models; do not substitute placeholder boxes in final walkthrough views.

The Smith machine and some existing multi-gyms have detailed meshes. Verify the proposed repeated quantities in the real 3D viewport and walkthrough, including cold loading and camera movement, before increasing them. An isolated thumbnail pass is not whole-gym performance qualification.

## Exclusions and Remaining Decisions

The following latest files are explicitly excluded by the user: `Exercise Machine.step`, `step file.stl`, and `WRACKSC.stp`. Do not retry or substitute them as part of building this layout without a new instruction.

Do not reinstate earlier unpublished files such as the scale-held bench press, leg-curl STL, or miniature treadmill STEP. Exclude earlier incomplete frame-only equipment from the counted operating-machine schedule unless the missing configuration is resolved.

No pool, sauna, court, or separately equipped class studio is included in this revision. The open functional area can be considered for small coached sessions, but no class size is claimed. Locker, toilet, shower, staff-room and reception fixtures need a separate catalog check; reserving their area is not a claim that their models or services are already available.

Before construction, confirm the proposed zone budget and quantities, the entrance/window concept, the pool-table lounge inclusion, and the support-room scope. Equipment installation instructions, structural loads, accessibility, egress, ventilation, and services require appropriate site-specific review before treating this as a buildable facility.

1. Create a separate blank layout named Private Gym, preserving Factory 02 and all existing layouts. Establish the 60 x 30 m concept shell and approved entrance orientation.
2. Block out zones, support rooms, and connected circulation. Check that gross allocations fit after partitions and door openings are represented.

7. Present the completed draft for review. Save only on explicit instruction; this plan does not authorize saved-layout changes.
